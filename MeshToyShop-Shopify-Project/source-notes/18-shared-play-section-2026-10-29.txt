## Rationale and limits

The prior shelf exposes choices. This section gives one concrete shared-use scenario so a parent can imagine how to use a toy. It connects emotional positioning to a specific product destination. Compared with a general brand-values banner, it provides an actionable play idea; compared with an unsourced testimonial, it makes no social-proof claim. It does not establish measured superiority to any competitor. This is an editorial invitation, not evidence that a child will become smarter, calmer or less screen-dependent.

Keep this compact: a second full hero or long educational essay would delay further shopping. If implemented, compare downstream product visits, add-to-cart and purchase rates among exposed sessions; watch scroll exits and page speed. No app or autoplay video is needed for the proposed static section.
