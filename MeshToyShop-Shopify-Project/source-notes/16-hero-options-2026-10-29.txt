## Blue-primary colour balance correction

The user clarified that **blue is Mesh's main colour** and asked for colour theory appropriate to toys. The prior V6 overused blue across ticker, logo, heading, body, link and button. [V7 balanced-blue mockup](../output/design-concepts/mesh-hero-blue-balanced-v7.png) keeps palette 08's cobalt as the primary interactive colour, cream as the dominant surface, navy for most text, and tangerine/yellow only as small warm signals. The peach Remote Control and mint Puzzles & Play cards add category distinction without turning the header into multiple competing coloured pills. The final pass removed those pills and excess decorative marks.

Rationale: blue provides a consistent navigation/action anchor; orange is its warm complement, useful at small scale for toy-category energy; yellow and pale mint add child-friendly warmth without competing with product photography. A large, multicolour header would make every route equally loud and weaken the main action. Text contrast for the proposed solid pairs: white on `#2354D7` ≈ 6.32:1, navy `#172B4D` on cream `#FFFCF5` ≈ 13.76:1. Tangerine `#FF8547` on cream is only ≈ 2.36:1, so it should remain decorative rather than carry small text. These are colour-pair calculations, not a full accessibility audit. The generated toy/category images are illustrative, not verified catalogue media. V7 is a proposal; no Shopify setting changed.

### V10 rejected: curve too deep; V11 measured correction

The user correctly noted that V10 exaggerated the curve. [V11 shallow-curve mockup](../output/design-concepts/mesh-mobile-shallow-curve-v11.png) reduces the dip and preserves the continuous cream-to-photo join. Approximate pixel boundary readings at left, centre and right establish: V7 reference [462, 485, 452] over 427 sampled pixels gives a centre dip of 6.6% relative to the endpoints' chord; V10 [341, 382, 341] over 402 pixels gives 10.2%; V11 [347, 372, 347] gives 6.2%. These are approximate colour-boundary measurements, not exact vector coordinates. V11 matches the reference's shallow character substantially more closely. Future mobile hero edits must preserve this shallow proportion (about 6% of width), not merely add any convex curve. A geometry-measurement requirement was added to AGENTS.md. Imagegen produced the correction; generated imagery and font approximations remain concept assets.
