## Content evidence and full answer drafts

IndiaMART educational page https://www.meshenterprise.in/learning-and-educational-toy.html describes Colour Coder as unpowered sliding-token play and sticker book as paper sticker activity. Shopify3D Maze Cube inspected earlier is a mechanical ball maze. The first answer is based on those formats, not a claim about all learning toys.

RC battery answer draft: It varies by model. Confirm the car and remote battery requirements separately, including whether batteries and a charging cable are supplied. If a listing is unclear, send us the product name before ordering.
Age answer draft: Check the manufacturer's age guidance and small-parts warnings for the exact toy. If the label or listing is unclear, contact us before choosing.
Box answer draft: Contents vary by product and version. Confirm the exact contents before ordering; props in playtime photos are not automatically included. Contact us if the product details leave anything unclear.

These drafts do not pretend missing catalog facts are known. Product pages still need verified age, power and box-content fields before launch; homepage FAQs cannot substitute for them. No unsupported delivery, returns, stock, prepaid or certification claims. Policy FAQs can be added after actual merchant policy confirmation.

## Rationale and test

Current primary competitor evidence: Smartivity Rocket Launcher page https://www.smartivity.in/products/rocket-launcher has specific box contents and questions about assembly/manuals/materials and missing parts. The useful lesson is to answer purchase doubts with product-specific facts, not borrow its guarantees. Mesh's mixed powered/unpowered assortment makes battery/age/contents checks relevant. A compact FAQ gives undecided shoppers a route to clarity without extending the page with another category shelf. No measured conversion superiority claimed. Track FAQ interaction, support usage and downstream purchases with section exposure; page length/scroll exits remain tradeoffs.

Built-in imagegen; [exact prompt](../output/design-concepts/buying-help-section-prompt.txt). When implemented use keyboard-operable accordion buttons, aria-expanded, associated answer panels, visible focus and44px minimum touch targets. No plugin is required by the proposed static content.
