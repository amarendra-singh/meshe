Hero curve geometry — regression-critical

Reference sampling: V7 centre dip≈6.6% of sampled width; rejected V10 ≈ 10.2%; V11≈6.2%. Measure the curve's central offset relative to the straight chord between its left/right join endpoints, not the overall panel height.

Implementation acceptance target:5.5–7.0% of mobile hero width. Example at 390 px content width:21.5–27.3 px dip, target 24.2 px. At 375 px:20.6–26.3 px. The cream panel overlaps the image continuously; photo top is not an independently rounded box. FAQ

A few things before playtime.

Choosing, ordering and getting ready to play.

Choosing a toy:

  1. How do I choose the right toy? Start with what they enjoy: puzzles, building, art or imaginative play. Check the toy’s age guidance before choosing. This exact answer is visible in M08.
  2. Can play support focus and patience? Draft: Matching a pattern or trying a puzzle gives children opportunities to stay with a task and try again. Follow their pace and keep play enjoyable; outcomes vary.
  3. Which toys work without batteries? Draft: Many puzzles, art activities and building sets work without batteries. Check each product’s power requirements and included accessories.
  4. What comes in the box? Draft: Contents vary by product. Check the listing for pieces, accessories and any batteries needed. Ask us if anything is unclear before ordering.
  5. Can you help me choose a gift? Draft: Tell us the child’s age, interests and your budget, and we can help you compare suitable options. Follow the manufacturer’s age guidance.

Orders & support — questions locked, answers require operational confirmation:

  1. When will my order arrive?
  2. How much does shipping cost?
  3. Can I pay on delivery?
  4. What if an item arrives damaged?
  5. Can I cancel or return an order?

Proposed answers are in docs 09, not approved merchant promises. Keep placeholders clearly identified in editor previews and do not publish empty or misleading answers.

06 — Assets, provenance and production gaps

Every included image is under assets/. asset-manifest.json records original location, package path, SHA-256, byte size, dimensions, role and readiness. asset-slots.json maps each image need to the available source or a specific missing standalone asset. asset-manifest.csv is the spreadsheet-readable inventory.

Four distinct asset classes

  1. Authoritative mockups: eight complete boards. Immutable visual references; never use them as full-page storefront backgrounds or clickable image maps.
  2. Supporting reference images: colour board, corrected category anatomy, older shallow curve, supplied header/menu crop and original attached detail images where accessible. Their filename/manifest states which detail they support. Never apply their obsolete parts.
  3. Catalog source images: original available product files. These are the closest evidence for exact toy identity, but may be low-resolution, collaged or contain supplier text. Their presence does not establish reproduction rights, safety certification, current stock or final product specs.
  4. Legacy references: older logo/category/campaign files, isolated and explicitly non-authoritative. They are included to prevent a builder from mistaking a convenient existing file for the selected design.
