- mesh-category-gadgets.png: friendly white robot with cobalt-blue eyes and wheeled base.

## Prompt specification

Each asset was generated separately as a premium ecommerce category illustration for Mesh Toy Shop, with photorealistic studio/product-style 3D rendering, soft natural lighting, tactile materials, centered complete objects, transparent background, small contact shadow and no text, logos, packaging, people or UI. The truck, puzzle and stacker shared cream/navy/cobalt branding and requested 12% safe margins. The robot requested a glossy black face, luminous blue oval eyes, a white shell, rounded child-friendly proportions and a three-quarter view facing left. All prompts explicitly described decorative category artwork rather than a specific product offered for sale.

## Deployment

All four originals saved locally, uploaded to Shopify Files, assigned with native image pickers, and verified on desktop/mobile on 26 October. See docs/10-design-conversion-pass.md.
