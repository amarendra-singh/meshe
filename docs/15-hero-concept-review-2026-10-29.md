# Optimized hero concept — review draft

Date: 29 October 2026. Image: [desktop and mobile concept](../output/design-concepts/mesh-optimized-hero-concept-2026-10-29.png). Generated with the built-in imagegen tool. This is an unapproved visual proposal; no Shopify theme setting or asset was changed.

## Conversion hypothesis

The current hero accurately names the catalogue but gives one broad “Shop all toys” path. This concept adds a concrete shared-play occasion, keeps “Shop all toys” as the main action, and makes “Explore RC toys” an immediate second route because 7 of the 17 published products are remote-control toys. Two category cards appear immediately below. On mobile, the buying action appears before the illustrative product scene, which should keep the next action visible without requiring a long scroll. These are design hypotheses, not measured conversion improvements.

## Proposed copy

- Eyebrow: **Play starts here**
- Heading: **A little less scrolling. A lot more playing.**
- Supporting line: **Discover RC adventures, clever puzzles and hands-on toys for the moments you share.**
- Primary action: **Shop all toys** → existing all-products collection.
- Secondary action: **Explore RC toys** → existing Remote Control collection.
- Category cards: **Remote Control** and **Puzzles & Play** → existing collections.

The hero does not state developmental outcomes, scarcity, reviews, delivery times or the unenforced prepaid offer. Before implementation, verify that the phrase “less scrolling” fits the merchant's chosen brand position and the products shown. The blue car and colorful track in the generated image are illustrative mockup objects, **not verified Mesh catalogue products**. Replace them with a permission-cleared image or verified product media before using this design in the live theme. The generated wordmark styling is also a concept and does not replace the current logo.
