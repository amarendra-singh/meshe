## Why these sections have separate jobs

Growth connects each activity to something a parent can observe: matching a pattern, trying a different construction, choosing colours or taking turns. FAQ addresses selection and fulfilment questions without repeating another product grid. Genuine WhatsApp stories could connect a customer's experience to the relevant toy; the layout is ready, but evidence is missing. These are conversion hypotheses, not measured sales improvements or proof of superiority over competitors.

The [AAP's play guidance](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/family-life/power-of-play/Pages/the-power-of-play-how-fun-and-games-help-children-thrive.aspx), checked 30 October 2026, discusses creative play, social interaction and emotional development, emphasizing caregivers' participation. This supports describing opportunities to practise and connect; it does not establish results for Mesh products. Do not claim improved IQ, cures for aggression or screen addiction, guaranteed attention changes, or superiority to other stores.

## Expanded FAQ answer draft

The first five are content drafts. Verify individual product listings before publishing. The last five are operational proposals, not confirmed Mesh policies.

1. **How do I choose the right toy?** Start with what they enjoy: puzzles, building, art or imaginative play. Check the toy's age guidance before choosing.
2. **Can play support focus and patience?** Matching a pattern or trying a puzzle gives children opportunities to stay with a task and try again. Follow their pace and keep play enjoyable; outcomes vary.
3. **Which toys work without batteries?** Many puzzles, art activities and building sets work without batteries. Check each product's power requirements and included accessories.
4. **What comes in the box?** Contents vary by product. Check the listing for pieces, accessories and any batteries needed. Ask us if anything is unclear before ordering.
5. **Can you help me choose a gift?** Tell us the child's age, interests and your budget, and we can help you compare suitable options. Follow the manufacturer's age guidance.
6. **When will my order arrive?** Proposed: We aim to dispatch within two working days. Estimated delivery is 3–8 working days after dispatch, depending on your PIN code. Tracking is shared once shipped. Validate warehouse and courier capability before adopting.
7. **How much does shipping cost?** Proposed: Shipping is calculated at checkout before payment. If adopting the competitor benchmark below, configure and disclose the exact threshold and exceptions before mentioning free shipping anywhere.
8. **Can I pay on delivery?** Proposed: COD is available for eligible orders and PIN codes, subject to confirmation. Available payment options and any charges appear at checkout. Validate actual availability; don't invent a COD surcharge or prepaid offer.
9. **What if an item arrives damaged?** Proposed: Contact us within 48 hours with your order number and photos of the item and packaging. Keep the packaging while we review the issue and arrange the appropriate resolution. Finalize replacement/refund procedure before publishing; do not describe this as extinguishing statutory rights.
10. **Can I cancel or return an order?** Proposed: Contact us promptly to request cancellation before dispatch. For an incorrect or defective item, contact support with the order details. Finalize return eligibility, collection costs, refund method and processing times before publishing the full policy.

## Competitor policy reference and adaptation

Its timing is not fully consistent. The proposed Mesh draft uses a clearer 48-hour initial reporting request and cancellation before dispatch, subject to operational and policy review.
