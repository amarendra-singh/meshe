# Store strategy, tool options, and delivery roadmap

Updated: 23 October 2026  
Status: Dawn build in progress; this roadmap remains the launch checklist. See [Dawn build status](05-dawn-build-status.md).

## 1. Strategy

Build a polished Shopify toy store that makes product selection straightforward and service expectations clear.

Professional quality should come from:
- A consistent brand system.
- Real, high-quality product photography.
- Useful product descriptions and specifications.
- Intuitive product discovery.
- Clear delivery and after-sales information.
- Genuine customer evidence.
- Reliable mobile purchase and checkout flows.
- Selective apps that solve actual requirements.

A paid theme or a large app stack is not a substitute for these fundamentals.

## 4. Product-page specification

Near purchase controls:
- Clear name and price.
- Actual gallery.
- Suitable age supported by product information.
- Main contents and key facts.
- Availability.
- Delivery/dispatch information.
- Concise service-policy summary.
- Quantity and relevant variants.
- Primary purchase action.
- Clear conditions for any promotion.

Detailed content:
- Materials.
- Dimensions and scale.
- What's included and excluded.
- Battery type/count and whether included.
- Assembly and supervision requirements.
- Care/use instructions.
- Relevant warnings.
- Supported certification information.
- Demonstration video.
- Actual customer reviews when collected.
- Product-specific FAQ.
- Relevant, age-appropriate complementary products.

Do not invent safety, developmental, educational, or certification claims. Confirm supplier/manufacturer documentation and applicable requirements before publishing them.

Mobile:
- Readable content.
- Comfortable touch targets.
- Gallery that does not bury product identity.
- Fixed purchase bar if supported and useful.
- No overlays obstructing essential controls.
- Test with actual product names and variant options.

## 5. Trust plan

### Product trust
- Accurate photos, dimensions, contents, and use.
- Genuine reviews and transparent review presentation.
- Evidence supporting materials and safety claims.
- No invented testimonials, fake purchase counts, artificial scarcity, or unsupported badges.

### Business trust
- Authentic brand story.
- Clear contact methods.
- Realistic support hours/reply expectations.
- Consistent identity and appropriate business details.
- Recognisable payment options when actually configured.

### Service trust
- Consistent terms across product pages, cart, FAQ, policy pages, and support responses.
- Realistic dispatch and transit estimates.
- Clear replacement/refund/cancellation process.
- Explicit product-specific exceptions.
- No service claims before fulfilment capability is confirmed.

### Checkout trust
- Consistent branding where Shopify permits.
- Transparent totals.
- Explained offer conditions.
- Working provider redirects and return paths.
- Correct confirmations and order emails.

For India, assess INR presentation, UPI/payment-provider options, COD suitability, PIN-code coverage, and support channels once the operational model is known. None is configured yet.

## 6. AOV and profit plan

AOV means average order value. An increase is useful only if the overall economics remain sound.

| Tactic | Example | Inputs needed | Success measure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Curated bundle | Age-appropriate toy/activity set | Product fit, inventory, cost, price | Bundle uptake and contribution per order |
| Complementary add-on | Compatible accessory or related activity | Compatibility and age suitability | Attachment rate and profit |
| Gift options | Wrapping/message | Operational cost and capacity | Uptake and contribution |
| Shipping threshold | Basket level that earns shipping subsidy | Basket distribution, margins, shipping cost | Incremental profit and conversion |
| Multipack / quantity offer | Party gifts or sibling sets | Demand, stock, discount economics | Units/order and contribution |
| Repeat purchase | Relevant follow-up recommendations | Consent, catalogue, purchase cycle | Repeat orders and retention |

Rules:
- The user selected a 15% prepaid discount with no cap or minimum; margin impact still needs checking before activation. No shipping threshold is selected.
- Recommend useful complements, not arbitrary extras.
- Keep cart recommendations limited.
- Make savings and conditions explicit.
- Review discount stacking.
- Track conversion and refunds alongside AOV.
- Prepaid incentives should be assessed against payment, COD, cancellation, and fulfilment economics; they are not automatically an AOV tactic.

Conceptual contribution calculation:
Order revenue after discounts, less product costs, payment/provider fees, packaging, fulfilment/shipping subsidy, and other variable costs. Returns and acquisition costs should be considered when evaluating overall profitability. Actual accounting definitions must be agreed before reporting.

## 9. Measurement plan

After launch, establish a baseline before interpreting changes.

Track:
- Sessions and conversion rate.
- Add-to-cart and checkout completion.
- AOV and units per order.
- Bundle and add-on attachment.
- Discount usage and stacking.
- Contribution profit.
- Returns, cancellations, and failed deliveries where available.
- Payment-method mix if relevant.
- Search queries and no-result searches.
- Repeat purchase.
- Mobile versus desktop performance.

Segment cautiously: traffic source, device, new/returning customer, and product category can change results. Do not attribute improvement to a colour or app without enough evidence.

## 11. Implementation safeguards

- Use the existing appropriate store/checkout once identified; do not create duplicates by assumption.
- Keep reference-site content and assets separate from Mesh assets.
- Do not infer approval from a mockup.
- Keep user-owned tabs and work intact.
- Before changing a selling store, prepare a reviewable theme version appropriate to the authorised scope.
- Recheck current app/theme charges before purchases.
- Use genuine product and customer evidence.
- Preserve the user's preference for discussion before implementation.
